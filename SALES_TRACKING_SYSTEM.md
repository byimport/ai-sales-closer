## ⚠️ ALERTS & BLOCKERS

### IF YOU SEE THESE, TAKE ACTION:

```
BLOCKER: Response rate drops below 10%
ACTION: 
1. Change email subject lines
2. Try new opening messages
3. Target different list of prospects

BLOCKER: Trial-to-paid drops below 40%
ACTION:
1. Improve onboarding flow
2. Add in-app tutorials
3. Offer discount for upgrade

BLOCKER: Demo scheduling taking > 3 days
ACTION:
1. Call prospects directly
2. Use shorter scheduling link
3. Offer 3-4 preset times

BLOCKER: Website traffic under 100/day
ACTION:
1. Increase ad spend
2. Post more on social media
3. Improve SEO (blog)

BLOCKER: Email opens under 20%
ACTION:
1. Improve subject lines
2. Send at different times
3. A/B test email templates
```
